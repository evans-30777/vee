# Skill: Design, Content, SEO & UX

## Design
VEE Agency currently uses the approved direction:
- dark/black foundation
- orange/red brand accents
- premium digital-agency feel
- strong typography
- restrained glass effects
- purposeful motion
- mobile-first responsive design

Keep the site visually coherent. Do not introduce unrelated visual systems.

## Content
Write for business owners and customers, not developers.

Prefer:
- specific
- concise
- benefit-led
- credible
- natural language

Avoid generic agency clichés and exaggerated promises.

Never invent proof.

## UX
Every important page should have a clear next action. Prioritize:
- understandability
- easy contact
- WhatsApp access
- readable pricing
- simple enquiry paths
- accessible navigation

## SEO/AEO
Treat SEO as part of implementation, not an afterthought.

Check where relevant:
- title/meta description
- canonical URL
- headings
- internal links
- schema/structured data
- sitemap
- robots
- image alt text
- location/service relevance
- crawlable content
- useful answer-oriented content

Do not make guaranteed ranking claims.

## Local targeting
Base: Syokimau, Machakos County, Kenya.
Primary emphasis: Nairobi, Machakos, Kajiado and Kiambu.
Service coverage: all of Kenya.

Do not imply VEE serves only the four named counties.
