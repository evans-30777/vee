About

One partner. Straight answers.

VEE Agency helps businesses get found, build trust and grow online through connected digital services — not isolated marketing activities that never add up.

How we work

Most Kenyan businesses don't have a marketing problem. They have a visibility problem: a website nobody finds, a Google listing nobody updated, and social pages that went quiet months ago. Each piece gets handled separately, by different people, and none of it pulls in the same direction.

VEE Agency works the other way round. We start with what your business actually needs to achieve, then build the website, search visibility, Google presence and campaigns as one connected system — so the work compounds instead of competing.

What you can expect

  • Direct contact. You deal with the person doing the work — no account managers, no handoffs, no being passed around.
  • Prices up front. Scope and cost in writing before anything starts.
  • Honest assessments. If something won't work for your business, we say so — including when the answer is that you don't need us yet.
  • Work you own. Your website, your accounts, your data. Always.
