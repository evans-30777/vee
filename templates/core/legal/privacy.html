This policy explains how VEE Agency collects, uses and protects personal information you provide through this website.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly through our enquiry form and newsletter signup: your name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, your indicated budget range and your message. We also record the IP address and browser information of form submissions to prevent spam and abuse.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, provide the services you request, and — if you subscribe — send you occasional updates. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Legal basis and consent

We process enquiry data on the basis of your request to be contacted. Newsletter emails are sent only to people who have subscribed, and every email includes a way to unsubscribe.

Retention

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to serve you and to keep proper business records. You may ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, and object to how we use it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us operate the website and communicate with you, such as our hosting provider and email provider. They are only permitted to use it to provide those services.

Security

We use appropriate technical measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
