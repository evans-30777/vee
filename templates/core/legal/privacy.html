Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly through our enquiry form and newsletter signup: your name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, your indicated budget range and your message. We also record the IP address and browser information of form submissions to prevent spam and abuse.

If you accept optional cookies, we also collect anonymous usage information about which pages are visited and how people arrive at the site. Nothing of this kind is collected unless you accept it. The Cookie Policy lists exactly what is set and by whom.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, provide the services you request, and — if you subscribe — send you occasional updates. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Legal basis and consent

We process enquiry data on the basis of your request to be contacted. Newsletter emails are sent only to people who have subscribed, and every email includes a way to unsubscribe. Analytics and advertising cookies are processed on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer.

Retention

We keep enquiry records for three years from your last contact with us, so we can serve you properly and keep accurate business records, after which they are deleted. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe. You may ask us to delete your information sooner at any time.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, object to how we use it, and ask for a copy of it in a portable form. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner if you believe we have handled your data improperly. You can reach the ODPC at odpc.go.ke.
