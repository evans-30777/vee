What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They let a website remember things between pages and visits.

Your choice

Where optional cookies are in use, you are asked before anything is loaded. Declining is offered exactly as prominently as accepting, and pressing Escape declines rather than dismissing the question.

You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer. Withdrawing consent stops any further loading and clears the optional cookies already stored in your browser. Anything already sent to Google or Meta before you withdrew cannot be recalled by us — you would need to contact them directly.

Other third-party connections

Fonts and all other assets on this site are served from our own server. This site makes no third-party requests at all unless you have accepted optional cookies.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of this website working, including the enquiry form.

Changes

If we add new cookies, we will update this page before they are used. The date at the top shows when this page last changed.
