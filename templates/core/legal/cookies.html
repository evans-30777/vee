This policy explains how this website uses cookies and similar technologies.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They let a website remember things between pages and visits.

Cookies we use

Essential cookies. These are required for the website to work — for example, to keep your session secure and to protect forms against cross-site request forgery. They cannot be switched off.

Analytics cookies. Where analytics are enabled, these help us understand which pages people visit so we can improve the site. They are not used to identify you personally.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of this website working, including the enquiry form.

Changes

If we add new cookies, we will update this page.
