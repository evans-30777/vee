This policy explains how this website uses cookies and similar technologies.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They let a website remember things between pages and visits.

Cookies we use

Essential cookies. These are required for the website to work — for example, to keep your session secure and to protect forms against cross-site request forgery. They cannot be switched off.

Analytics cookies. Where analytics are enabled, these help us understand which pages people visit so we can improve the site. They are not used to identify you personally, and they are only set if you accept them.

Your choice

If this site is using analytics, you will see a banner asking whether you accept them the first time you visit. Nothing beyond the essential cookies described above is loaded until you choose — and declining is always available alongside accepting, never buried.

If you decline, the site works exactly as it does otherwise. You lose no functionality.

You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer. Withdrawing consent stops any further analytics loading and clears the analytics cookies already stored in your browser.

Managing cookies

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of this website working, including the enquiry form.

Changes

If we add new cookies, we will update this page.
