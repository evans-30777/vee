SEO — traditional search

Clean structure, fast loading, proper headings, local keywords and a working sitemap, so Google can index and rank every page.

AEO — answer engines

Direct answers and FAQ schema so your business gets quoted in featured snippets and voice results, not buried on page two.

GEO — AI search

Clear, factual, well-structured content that AI assistants can read and cite when someone asks them to recommend a business like yours.

The process

  2. Structure

    Pages, content and search structure are planned before any design work, so nothing important gets bolted on later.

  3. Build

    We design and build mobile-first, review it with you, and refine until it reflects your business properly.

  4. Launch & support

    We go live, submit your site to search engines, and stay available for management if you want it handled.
