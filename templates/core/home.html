01 The gap

Being online isn't the same as being found

Most businesses already have something online. The real question is what a customer finds in the thirty seconds after they first hear your name.

They check before they call

Someone hears about you and looks you up first. That check happens whether or not you planned for it, and what it turns up is the impression you get.

Pieces that don't talk to each other

A website built once, a Facebook page updated sometimes, a Google listing set up years ago. Each one half-works, and none of them pull in the same direction.

Effort with no way to read it

Posting regularly, maybe boosting a post now and then. Plenty of activity — but no clear line between what you do and what actually brings in business.

None of that is a marketing problem. It's a visibility and structure problem — and structure is fixable.

04 How it fits together

The VEE Digital Growth System

Five stages, in order. Each one works on its own — run together, each makes the next one cheaper and more effective.

  1. 01

    Get Found

    Show up when someone searches for what you sell — on Google, on Maps, and in the AI answers people increasingly rely on.

  2. 02

    Look Professional

    A website and profiles that hold up to the check a customer makes before they commit to talking to you.

  3. 03

    Stay Visible

    Planned, consistent content, so you are still there next month — not only in the week you launched.

  4. 04

    Reach More People

    Targeted campaigns, once the foundations can actually convert the traffic they bring. Ads before that is spending to be ignored.

  5. 05

    Turn Interest Into Action

    Clear paths to call, message or enquire, so attention turns into a conversation instead of a bounce.

This is why the work is sold as packages rather than one-off pushes — the stages compound, and doing one in isolation usually wastes the others.

Common questions

Straight answers before you call

How much does a website cost in Kenya?

VEE Agency builds websites from KES 30,000. That base covers a professional, mobile-friendly, SEO-ready site. More complex builds — e-commerce stores or hotel booking systems — are quoted individually because the scope is larger.

Which areas in Kenya do you serve?

We are based in Syokimau, Machakos County, and work closely with businesses in Nairobi, Machakos, Kajiado and Kiambu. Work is delivered remotely across all 47 counties, so your location is not a barrier.

What is included in a monthly growth package?

Monthly packages start at KES 15,000 and combine the services a business needs to stay visible — search optimisation, Google Business Profile management, social media and reporting. Each package lists exactly what it includes on the Packages page.

What type of website does my business need?

It depends on what the site has to do. A portfolio site suits professionals who need to show work and be contacted easily. An e-commerce site suits businesses selling products online. A booking site suits hotels, salons and venues taking reservations. We recommend the right type during your free audit.
