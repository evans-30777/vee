Common questions

Straight answers before you call

How much does a website cost in Kenya?

VEE Agency builds websites from KES 30,000. That base covers a professional, mobile-friendly, SEO-ready site. More complex builds — e-commerce stores or hotel booking systems — are quoted individually because the scope is larger.

Which areas in Kenya do you serve?

We are based in Syokimau, Machakos County, and work closely with businesses in Nairobi, Machakos, Kajiado and Kiambu. Work is delivered remotely across all 47 counties, so your location is not a barrier.

What is included in a monthly growth package?

Monthly packages start at KES 15,000 and combine the services a business needs to stay visible — search optimisation, Google Business Profile management, social media and reporting. Each package lists exactly what it includes on the Packages page.

What type of website does my business need?

It depends on what the site has to do. A portfolio site suits professionals who need to show work and be contacted easily. An e-commerce site suits businesses selling products online. A booking site suits hotels, salons and venues taking reservations. We recommend the right type during your free audit.
